## Key Features of AI-Powered Insights:

1. **Specific Predictions**: Real percentages and probabilities
2. **Deep Reasoning**: AI explains WHY patterns were identified  
3. **Contextual Awareness**: Considers demographics, history, and current state
4. **Actionable Recommendations**: Specific, measurable actions
5. **Confidence Scoring**: AI assesses its own prediction reliability
6. **Pattern Recognition**: Discovers hidden correlations humans miss
7. **Personalization**: Tailored to individual behavioral fingerprints
8. **Predictive Power**: Forecasts future behavior based on patterns
